Blank departments below often mean "not captured in the source extraction," not necessarily "not taught." Treat this as a first draft to validate against the registrar's official section roster, not a final source of truth.

## Data Confidence & Gaps

- **Section identity model:** a section is uniquely identified by (grade, name), not name alone — the same saint/Jesuit name (e.g. "Ignatius," "Xavier") is reused as an independent section in multiple grades. This matches the entity model in [[JHS Scheduling Constraints]] §1 and is why, for example, "Ignatius" appears in both the Grade 7 and Grade 8 tables above as two different sections.
- **Extraction reliability varies by sheet.** CLE and MAPEH's source PDFs did not tag grade level as cleanly as Filipino, Science, Mathematics, Social Studies, and TLE did — several CLE and MAPEH cells above may be misattributed to the wrong grade. Before this directory is used to seed a production database, each department chair should re-confirm their own department's rows against this table.
- **Grade 10 is substantially under-covered.** Only TLE and Social Studies show regular G10 teaching loads; the other five departments show no G10 subject-teaching rows at all in their plantillas, only a scattering of G10 Class Moderator assignments. Before building the Grade 10 schedule, confirm with the registrar whether: (a) G10 plantillas for the other five departments simply weren't submitted/digitized yet, (b) G10 uses a different set of subject teachers not reflected in these sheets, or (c) G10 truly has fewer of these subjects (e.g. some subjects end at G9).
- **Blank cells are not evidence of "no class"** — treat every blank in the tables above as "unconfirmed," not "not offered."
