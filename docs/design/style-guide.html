Design System

The visual identity for the teaching-load platform — cobalt chrome, canary signal, and the Anuncio frame, tuned from loud announcement posters into a calm workspace for administrators and department chairs.

Institutional AtenistaLight-firstBlade · Tailwindv1 — 2026-08-07

Direction

Loud where it announces, quiet where you work

The source materials are maximalist marketing posters. This is a data-dense admin tool. The identity resolves that tension by putting the brand's energy in the chrome — the navy sidebar, uppercase page titles, primary actions, and status — while the tables and forms stay white and legible. Blue is identity, white is workspace, and canary yellow is the rare, deliberate highlight.
